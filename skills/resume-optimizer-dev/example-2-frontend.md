## Real World Notes

As an early-career developer:
- Your lack of years is okay—you have projects and technologies that matter
- Testing knowledge is huge differentiator for junior roles
- Design collaboration (Figma, design systems) shows maturity
- Full-stack ownership and deployment knowledge is valuable
- Performance optimization mindset (React.memo, memoization) shows depth beyond "just getting it working"

Focus on what you *have done*, not what you haven't learned yet.
